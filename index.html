The CSE Cheese Society

The CSE Cheese Society was founded in 2024, when a seminar ended early and a cheese plate did not.

Our mission is simple: taste widely, take notes, and never let processed cheese food into the building.

Cheese Fact

The word "cheese" comes from the Latin caseus, which also gives us the word "casein," the main milk protein.
